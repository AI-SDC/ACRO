Ontology alignment self-assessment

Compare your manual statistical disclosure control processes with the StatBarn SDC ontology as captured by the JSON files packaged with ACRO. For each activity, choose the closest analysis term, review its linked StatBarn category, risks and checks, and record how your process addresses each check. Click any term name to see its definition.

This is a guide for human assessment, not a certification or safety decision. Ontology links describe potential relationships and may not cover every local risk or procedure. Everything you enter stays in this browser; nothing is sent to a server. Use the download button to save a JSON copy.
